Fle Cookies

Ordering app for a cookie brand — brand audit, flow, UI and prototype

The app doesn't replace the chat. It writes the message.

Fle Cookies is a home bakery that sells entirely through WhatsApp, where every order used to begin as a blank chat. I designed a six-screen mobile flow that ends by composing the message for the customer — products, quantities, total and delivery details already written — with a review step that shows the exact text before it is sent. The system is built from the brand's own asset kit rather than its website, which turned out to be a reinterpretation that had broken the brand's already-accessible black-on-colour lockup.

04 — the build

rebuilding the landing page

The design work surfaced a problem in the live site, so I rebuilt it. Audited with a DOM-level contrast and target-size pass:

  • Contrast fixed. All five WhatsApp CTAs had white text on #25D366 — 1.98:1, failing every WCAG level. Now black on the same green.
  • Palette realigned to the brand kit instead of the website's reinterpretation.
  • Real logotype in place of a script font imitating it.
  • Illustrated products replace emoji — one recoloured cookie per flavour.
  • Mobile navigation fixed. The old page hid the nav below 600 px with nothing in its place; now there is a fixed order bar.
  • Per-product WhatsApp links pre-fill the flavour, instead of five identical empty messages.
  • Skip link, visible focus rings and prefers-reduced-motion on the marquee.

The brand had already solved contrast: every flyer and post uses black on colour, a lockup that passes AA across the whole palette. White — what the website used — fails on every single brand colour. The fix was to follow the brand, not to invent.
